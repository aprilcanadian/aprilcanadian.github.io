🐶 Privacy Policy

Duke: The Autistic Book of Lists Virtual Pet
Effective date: October 16, 2026

We collect nothing.

Duke does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Everything stays on your device.

Your checklists, Duke Dollars, Duke's care stats, and all app progress are saved only in your device's own local storage. We never see it, and it never leaves your device. If you uninstall the app, that data is removed with it.

Children's privacy

Duke is designed to be safe for the whole family, including children. Since we collect no information from anyone, there is nothing to misuse. There is no chat, no user-to-user contact, and no way to share personal details inside the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
